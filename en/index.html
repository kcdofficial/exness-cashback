What is Exness Rebate and how does it work?

Exness Rebate is a cashback program where you receive 100% refund of the partner commission that Exness pays us. Every time you trade, a portion of the spread you pay becomes partner commission, and we return up to 100% to your account automatically every day.

Absolutely safe. Your Exness trading account is registered under your own name. We only act as a partner (IB) who refers you. We have no access to withdraw or change your account password. Exness is a regulated broker with high security standards.

No. You trade with exactly the same spreads, execution, and account types as any other Exness client. The cashback comes from our partner commission, not from your account. Your spreads remain the same.

When and how do I receive cashback?

Cashback is credited automatically to your Exness account every day. Every payout is verifiable directly in your Exness dashboard — transparent on every order. No manual claim needed.

Does the rebate apply to Exness Pro and Standard accounts?

Yes, the Exness 100% Auto Rebate program applies to all account types, including Exness Standard, Exness Pro (ECN), and Standard Cent accounts, as well as for all instruments like Forex, XAUUSD (Gold), and Crypto.

Yes! Just contact Exness live chat and ask to "Change Partner" to our link. The process is easy and your account remains the same. See the complete guide in the "How to Claim" section above.

We are an official Exness partner (Introducing Broker). We have no access to your funds or trading account — Exness credits the cashback, and you can verify everything on Exness's side. 100% safe and trusted.

How much cashback do I get?

You get up to 100% cashback from the partner commission. For trading 1 standard lot, you can get up to $14 cashback. For cent accounts, the proportion is the same. All cashback is automatically credited to your trading account.
