What is Cashback?

Cashback (rebate) is a partial refund of your trading costs: part of the spread you pay becomes a partner commission that Exness pays us — and we return up to 100% of that commission to you.

Example: you trade and pay a spread — e.g. $20 per lot. Exness shares part of it with us as partner commission, and up to 100% of that commission goes back into your own Exness account daily. Your spread never changes.

  • Zero impact on your trading results or account
  • Transparent system, verifiable directly via Exness
